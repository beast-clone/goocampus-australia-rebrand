The Program

End-to-End
AMC Guidance

The Post Graduate Consulting Program brings coaching, portfolio, visa, accommodation and interview prep under a single roof — so nothing about your move is left to guesswork.

(01) AMC MCQ Preparation

Learn from Australia-based faculty who specialise in training IMGs for the competitive AMC MCQ — 90+ hours across three months, with weekly mentorship and revision strategy.

  • Overview session with a senior physician
  • Clarity on what to study, and what to skip
  • Proven study materials and MCQ bank
  • Live, discussion-based Q&A sessions
(02) AMC Clinical Preparation

Clear the demanding AMC clinical exam with a live, online intensive built closely around the official format, plus pre-recorded lectures across every domain you will be tested on.

General Medicine Obstetrics Psychiatry Gynaecology Paediatrics Physical Exam
(03) Mentorship & Job Support

Learn from Australia-based physicians who have completed the pathway themselves, with personalised mentorship at every stage.

  • One-to-one, personalised mentorship
  • A CV and cover letter built to Australian standards
  • A clear read on the selection process
  • Comprehensive communication-skills training
(04) Portfolio Building

Build a job-ready portfolio through a structured, fast-tracked program that helps you earn valuable CPD points.

  • ALS course — Australia-recognised certification
  • Audit and QIP — quality-improvement projects
  • Conference talks and research publication support
  • BMJ CPD access — ongoing learning credits

FAQs

Frequently Asked
Questions

How long does the AMC pathway take?

Coaching runs about three months for the AMC MCQ, with clinical preparation and portfolio work alongside. Total time to a posting depends on your exam attempts, registration and job applications — we map a realistic timeline in your first consultation.

What do I need to get started?

A recognised primary medical qualification, your current documents, and a free consultation with our team. We assess eligibility and give you an honest read on your fastest realistic route.

Who teaches the coaching sessions?

Australia-based physicians who specialise in training International Medical Graduates — many of whom completed the AMC Standard Pathway themselves.

Do you help with visa and job placement?

Yes. The end-to-end track covers visa support, accommodation guidance, a CV built to Australian standards, interview preparation and application strategy through to your first posting.
