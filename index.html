Why Australia

A country that actively wants international medical graduates.

Growing demand and real support

An ageing population and steady healthcare growth mean high demand for doctors, especially GPs and rural specialists. Government incentives for regional roles open strong opportunities for IMGs across specialties, research and management.

Rewarding careers

Competitive remuneration, clear progression and one of the most respected healthcare systems in the world.

Balanced lifestyle

Strong protections around working hours and leave make room for a life outside the hospital, not just inside it.
