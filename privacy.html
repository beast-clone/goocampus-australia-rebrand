Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store and disclose your personal information — and the rights you have over it under Australian law.

Last updated: 23 August 2026 Applies to: GooCampus Australia website and services

01

Who we are

This website is operated by GooCampus Edu Solutions Private Limited, an Indian company trading as GooCampus and, for the services described on this site, as GooCampus Australia (“GooCampus”, “we”, “us”, “our”).

We provide education consulting and coaching to International Medical Graduates pursuing the Australian Medical Council (AMC) Standard Pathway. We are not a university, a government body, or a migration agent, and we are not affiliated with the Australian Medical Council.

Our registered office is at #138/6, Ground Floor, 10th Main Road, 6th A Cross, Sadashiv Nagar, RMV Extension, Behind SBI Bank, Bangalore, Karnataka – 560080, India.

If you have any question about this policy or about how we handle your information, contact us at info@goocampus.in.

02

Scope of this policy

This policy applies to personal information we collect through this website and through the related services, enquiries, consultations, coaching programs and events we offer to prospective and current clients in connection with the Australian pathway.

Because we offer services to individuals in Australia, we handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). As an Indian company, we are also subject to India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Where those obligations differ, we apply the standard more protective of you.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not submit your information to us.

03

What information we collect

Information you give us

We collect personal information you provide voluntarily — when you book a free consultation, enquire about a program, message us, or enrol. This may include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your country of residence and preferred contact method
  • Details of your medical qualification, university, year of graduation and current registration status
  • Which stage of the AMC pathway you are at, and which exam or program you are interested in
  • The content of any message you send us

Some of this — for example details of your medical qualifications and professional registration — may be treated as sensitive information under the Privacy Act. We collect it only with your consent, only where it is reasonably necessary to advise you on the pathway, and we do not use it for any other purpose.

Information collected automatically

When you visit this website, our hosting provider records standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, referring page and the date and time of your visit. This is used to keep the site secure and working, and does not by itself identify you.

Payment information

We do not collect or process any payment on this website. There is no checkout, no card form and no payment processor running on these pages. If you choose to enrol in a paid program, payment is arranged separately and handled by our finance team or a third-party payment provider, who will tell you at that time how your payment data is handled. We never ask for card details by email or over WhatsApp.

04

How we use your information

We use personal information only for the purpose you gave it to us for, and for directly related purposes you would reasonably expect. Specifically:

  • To respond to your enquiry and schedule your consultation
  • To assess which AMC pathway program suits your stage and qualifications, and to advise you accordingly
  • To deliver coaching, materials and support if you enrol
  • To send you service messages about a program you have enrolled in
  • To send you information about our programs where you have asked us to, or where you would reasonably expect it — you can opt out at any time
  • To improve our website and services, and to keep them secure
  • To meet our legal, accounting and record-keeping obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it to make automated decisions that produce a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

05

Who we share it with

We disclose personal information only where it is necessary to run our services, and only to parties bound to protect it. These include:

  • Form and scheduling providers. Our consultation and enquiry forms are hosted by Fillout, which receives and stores your submission on our behalf.
  • Messaging platforms. If you contact us via WhatsApp, your message and phone number are handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own privacy terms as well as ours.
  • Email, storage and workflow providers we use to run the business and hold client records.
  • Professional advisers — accountants, auditors and lawyers — where required.
  • Government or regulatory bodies where we are required by law to disclose.

We do not share your information with universities, hospitals, recruiters or any third party for their own marketing purposes.

06

Overseas disclosure

Please read this section carefully if you are in Australia.

GooCampus is based in India, and our team accesses and stores client information there. Some of the service providers we use — including our form provider and messaging platforms — store data in the United States or other countries.

This means that if you give us your personal information, it will be disclosed to and held by recipients outside Australia, principally in India and the United States. By submitting your information, you consent to that overseas disclosure.

You should be aware that overseas recipients may be subject to laws that differ from the Privacy Act, and that in some cases you may not be able to seek redress under the Privacy Act in respect of an overseas recipient. We take reasonable steps to ensure the providers we use have appropriate security and confidentiality protections in place.

07

Cookies

This website uses only what is necessary to serve the pages and load fonts. We do not currently run advertising pixels or third-party analytics on these pages.

Third-party services embedded in or linked from the site — such as our form provider — may set their own cookies when you interact with them, governed by their own policies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may then not work as intended.

If we add analytics or advertising tracking in future, we will update this policy before doing so.

08

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it, or as long as the law requires.

  • Enquiries that do not proceed: generally up to 24 months from your last contact with us, so we can pick up the conversation if you return.
  • Client records: for the duration of your program and then as required for our tax, accounting and legal obligations.

When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to destroy it or permanently de-identify it. You can ask us to delete your information sooner — see below.

09

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. These include serving this site over HTTPS, restricting access to client records to staff who need it, and choosing reputable service providers.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach occurs that is likely to result in serious harm to you, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

10

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Ask what we hold. Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct it. Ask us to update anything inaccurate, out of date or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete it, where we are not required to keep it.
  • Opt out of marketing. Use the unsubscribe link, reply STOP, or email us — we will action it promptly.
  • Withdraw consent to our handling of your information, understanding that we may then be unable to continue advising you.
  • Deal with us anonymously or by pseudonym for a general enquiry, where it is lawful and practicable for us to do so.

Email info@goocampus.in to make any of these requests. We will respond within a reasonable period, ordinarily within 30 days. We do not charge for making a request. If we refuse access or correction, we will tell you why in writing.

11

Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, please tell us first so we can put it right. Write to info@goocampus.in with the details. We will acknowledge your complaint and aim to resolve it within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner:

12

Our admission practices

We do not engage in direct management seat admissions. Our function is consulting: we provide advice, support, information, documentation assistance, training and study programs to help you pursue your own application. We do not guarantee an exam result, a registration outcome, a job, or a visa.

13

Unauthorised payment requests

If anyone requests cash payments, or payments to any account or location other than those confirmed in writing by our Bangalore head office, do not pay — contact us immediately on the details below. GooCampus Edu Solutions Pvt Ltd accepts no responsibility for payments made to individuals or entities impersonating our team.
